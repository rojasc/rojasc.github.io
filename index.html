I build backend systems that move money.

I've spent the last seven years on CK Money — Credit Karma's banking product, now part of Intuit — building the digital wallet, check deposit, Paycheck Advance, and the TurboTax refund advance flow, while helping pull our banking monolith apart into event-driven services. Based in Charlotte. Always happy to talk shop about any of it.
